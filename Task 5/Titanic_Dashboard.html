⚠ Data quality caveat — read first: Survived matches Sex with zero exceptions across all 418 rows (every female = survived, every male = did not). That is the exact fingerprint of Kaggle's gender_submission.csv naive benchmark, not individually verified outcomes (see DATA_QUALITY_REPORT.md). Every chart below that is driven by Survived is therefore really showing the sex mix of each group, not a genuine survival effect — chart titles and notes are worded accordingly.
