4. Insights

1

Technology drives the most revenue, but Office Supplies is just as profitable.

Technology leads sales at $836,154, but its margin isn't actually any better than Office Supplies. Both categories convert roughly 17.2% of sales into profit, so Technology's lead comes mainly from a handful of high-ticket items like copiers and video-conferencing units, not from stronger performance across the board.

2

Furniture sells well but barely makes money.

Furniture is the #2 category by revenue at $742,000, but its profit margin is only 2.5%, compared to roughly 17.2% for the other two categories. That works out to just $18,451 in profit on those $742,000 of sales.

3

Tables and Bookcases are actively losing money.

Drilling into sub-categories shows why: Tables lost $17,725 overall and Bookcases lost $3,473, both sold at a net loss despite real revenue behind them. That's the direct cause of Furniture's weak margin above, and a clear place to review pricing or discounting.

4

Sales are heavily seasonal, peaking at year-end.

November is the strongest calendar month overall, with $352,461 in sales aggregated across all four years, and the single best month on record is 2018-11 at $118,448. It looks like a holiday-driven demand spike worth planning inventory and promotions around.

5

The West region outsells the South region by nearly 2x.

West and East together generate over 60% of total sales (31.6% and 29.5% respectively), while South trails at just $391,722, or 17.1% of the total. That gap looks like a real opportunity for targeted growth efforts in the South region.
